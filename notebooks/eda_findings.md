# EDA Findings

Verified against the supplied `data/raw/bangalore_traffic.csv` on 2026-10-22. Its MD5 matches the project's DVC pointer. The raw file has 8,936 rows and 16 columns, with daily dates from 2022-01-01 through 2024-08-09. All 952 dates in that range are present. The EDA working table has 19 columns after adding three calendar fields; those are not extra raw columns.

The local file was supplied as `Banglore_traffic_Dataset.csv` and is attributed in project notes to Bangalore's Traffic Pulse (Preetham Gouda, Kaggle). The local file and its DVC hash were checked; the original dataset source and listing were not independently verified. Dataset license status: **unverified**.

## Data quality

There are no blank cells, exact duplicate rows, or duplicate `(Date, Road/Intersection Name)` pairs. The date parses cleanly. Traffic Volume ranges from 4,233 to 72,039 (mean 29,236; median 27,600). The 1.5 × IQR rule flags 24 high observations (0.27%); this is a statistical flag, not evidence of an error, and the feature pipeline retains all 24.

## Patterns

Koramangala has the highest average traffic volume (40,832) and Electronic City the lowest (16,347). A group-mean calculation explains 34.1% of in-sample volume variance by area and 34.2% by road. These descriptive figures are not held-out model scores. Area counts are uneven: Indiranagar has 1,720 rows (19.2%) and Electronic City 552 (6.2%).

Average volume is 29,297 on weekdays and 29,084 on weekends (-0.7%). Monthly averages range from 28,830 to 29,742. Weather averages are similar across categories; Windy is least frequent (428 rows, 4.8%). These differences are descriptive, not causal effects.

Environmental Impact has Pearson correlation 1.000 with Traffic Volume. Congestion Level (0.837), Travel Time Index (0.698), and Road Capacity Utilization (0.653) are also strongly associated with the target and measured for the same observation. Keep these fields out of model inputs to avoid leakage.

## Plot review

The existing area, day-of-week, weather, month, and correlation plots are relevant and were regenerated by the EDA script from the verified CSV. A traffic-volume histogram was added to show the overall target distribution. The correlation plot is descriptive and includes same-time measurements; it must not be read as permission to use them for prediction.

## Modeling limits

Location is the clearest descriptive pattern in this daily dataset. The file can support historical daily traffic-volume modeling, but it does not contain time-of-day, queue length, signal phase, or a future target. Same-day weather is usable for a real forecast only if replaced by information available before prediction. Future queue forecasting and adaptive signal control require suitable timestamped observations, such as later SUMO simulation data tracked separately.
